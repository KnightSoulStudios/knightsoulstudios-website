About

An independent studio
building for everyone

Knight Soul Studios was founded on a single belief - that making games should be accessible to everyone, regardless of experience or background.

We build our own engine, our own tools, and our own games. Everything we create is designed so that a solo developer with one skill and a AAA studio with a full team can both do their best work.

Level playing field
Procedural worlds, visual scripting, and smart presets mean every developer can contribute what they are good at.
Fair commercial terms
1% royalty. No upfront cost. No tiers. The best commercial terms in the industry.
Built for the genre
Purpose-built systems for open world survival. Not a generic engine stretched to fit.
All platforms
PC, Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo. Supported as standard from day one.
